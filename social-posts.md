## LinkedIn (attach program.png)

The full program for the 5th MMFM Workshop at #CVPR2026 is here! 🎉

"What is Next in Multimodal Foundation Models?" takes place on **June 3, 2026 (afternoon)** at the Colorado Convention Center in Denver (Rooms 3A–3D). Building on four successful editions, MMFM brings together researchers from academia and industry to explore the frontiers of multimodal foundation models across vision, language, speech, and beyond.

🎤 Keynote speakers & times:
• 1:05 PM — Serena Yeung-Levy (Stanford University)
• 1:35 PM — Vincent Sitzmann (MIT EECS)
• 2:05 PM — Trevor Darrell (UC Berkeley)
• 4:00 PM — Hadar Averbuch-Elor (Cornell University)
• 4:30 PM — Shuai Bai (Alibaba, Qwen-VL)

🗓️ Also on the agenda:
• Poster session & coffee break — 2:35 PM
• Panel discussion: "What is Next in Multimodal Foundation Models?" — 5:00 PM

📌 Full schedule & details:
👉 https://mmfm-workshop.github.io

Looking forward to seeing the community in Denver!
